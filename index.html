About Me

// engineering profile

I'm an AI Engineer with 3+ years of software engineering experience building production-grade AI applications, distributed backend systems, and cloud-native platforms.

My work spans multi-agent orchestration, multimodal retrieval-augmented generation, autonomous desktop agents, Kubernetes operations at scale, and AI evaluation pipelines. I'm currently building IoT video-streaming infrastructure for ~100K devices and a multimodal manufacturing AI assistant at Kamikaze Solution.

I'm particularly interested in systems where AI is one part of a larger engineering picture — retrieval, state, APIs, security, observability, and deployment all matter as much as the model itself.
